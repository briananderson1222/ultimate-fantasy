## Getting Started

### Download and Installation

**iOS (App Store)**
- Search for "Ultimate Fantasy" in the App Store
- Tap "Get" to download and install
- Requires iOS 14.0 or later

**Android (Google Play)**
- Search for "Ultimate Fantasy" in Google Play Store
- Tap "Install" to download
- Requires Android 8.0 (API level 26) or later

### Creating Your Account

1. **Open the App**: Tap the Ultimate Fantasy icon on your home screen
2. **Sign Up**: Tap "Create Account" on the welcome screen
3. **Enter Details**: Provide your email, username, and password
4. **Verify Email**: Check your email for a verification link
5. **Complete Profile**: Add your name and timezone preferences

### Logging In

1. **Enter Credentials**: Use your email and password
2. **Biometric Login**: Enable Face ID/Touch ID or fingerprint for quick access
3. **Stay Logged In**: Toggle "Remember Me" for convenience

## League Management

### Joining a League

**Via Invite Code**
1. Tap "Join League" on the home screen
2. Enter the 6-character invite code from your commissioner
3. Choose your team name
4. Confirm and join

**Via Invitation Link**
1. Tap the invitation link in your email or message
2. The app will open automatically
3. Confirm you want to join the league

### Creating a League

1. **Tap "Create League"** on the home screen
2. **Choose Sport**: Select MLB, NFL, or WNBA
3. **League Settings**:
   - League name and type (Standard, Keeper, Dynasty)
   - Number of teams (4-20)
   - Draft type (Snake, Auction, Linear)
   - Scoring system
4. **Invite Friends**: Share the invite code or send direct invitations

### League Dashboard

**Quick Stats**
- Your team record and ranking
- Next opponent and matchup
- Recent league activity

**Navigation Tabs**
- **Standings**: Current league rankings
- **Teams**: View all league teams and rosters
- **Schedule**: Upcoming matchups
- **Activity**: Recent trades, waiver moves, and news

## Draft Experience

### Joining Your Draft

1. **Draft Notification**: You'll receive a push notification 30 minutes before your draft
2. **Join Early**: Enter the draft room up to 30 minutes before start time
3. **Check Connection**: Ensure you have a stable internet connection

### Draft Room Interface

**Your Information Panel**
- Team name and draft position
- Time remaining for current pick
- Your next pick number

**Player List**
- **Available Players**: Searchable and filterable
- **Your Queue**: Pre-rank players you want
- **Recent Picks**: See what others have selected

**Draft Board**
- View all completed picks by round
- See team needs and strategies
- Track position runs

### Making Picks

**Manual Selection**
1. **Browse Players**: Use search and filters to find players
2. **Select Player**: Tap on the player you want
3. **Confirm Pick**: Tap "Draft Player" button
4. **Pick Made**: Player is added to your roster

**Auto-Pick Setup**
1. **Queue Players**: Drag players to your draft queue
2. **Set Preferences**: Choose position priorities
3. **Enable Auto-Pick**: Toggle on for automatic selection
4. **Backup Strategy**: Set what happens if queue is empty

### Draft Features

**Real-time Updates**
- Live pick notifications
- Timer countdown
- Chat with league members

**Player Information**
- Season projections and rankings
- Recent news and injury updates
- Advanced statistics

**Draft Strategy Tools**
- Position scarcity indicators
- Value-based rankings
- Bye week considerations

## Lineup Management

### Setting Your Lineup

**Accessing Lineups**
1. Tap "Lineup" in the bottom navigation
2. Select the week you want to manage
3. View current starting lineup and bench

**Making Changes**
1. **Tap a Position**: Select the position you want to change
2. **Choose Player**: Pick from eligible players on your roster
3. **Confirm**: Tap "Start Player" to make the change
4. **Submit**: Don't forget to submit your lineup before the deadline

### Lineup Optimization

**AI Recommendations**
1. Tap the "Optimize" button in your lineup
2. Choose your strategy (Balanced, High Ceiling, Safe Floor)
3. Review suggested changes
4. Apply recommendations with one tap

**Player Projections**
- View projected points for each player
- See matchup difficulty ratings
- Check weather and injury reports

### Weekly Management

**Lineup Deadlines**
- **Daily Sports**: 1 hour before first game of the day
- **Weekly Sports**: Tuesday at 1:00 PM ET
- **Notifications**: Get reminders 2 hours and 30 minutes before deadline

**Benchwarming**
- Players on your bench don't score points
- Use bench for bye weeks and poor matchups
- Consider waiver wire for better options

## Trading

### Proposing Trades

**Finding Trade Partners**
1. **Tap "Trades"** in the bottom navigation
2. **Browse Teams**: View other team rosters
3. **Identify Needs**: Look for teams that need your players' positions

**Creating Proposals**
1. **Select Teams**: Choose the team you want to trade with
2. **Add Players**: Select players you're offering
3. **Request Players**: Choose what you want in return
4. **Add Message**: Include a note explaining your thinking
5. **Send Proposal**: Submit for the other team to review

### Managing Trade Offers

**Incoming Offers**
- Receive push notifications for new trade proposals
- Review player details and projections
- Accept, reject, or counter the offer

**Your Proposals**
- Track status of trades you've sent
- Modify or cancel pending offers
- See when trades are viewed by recipients

### Trade Analysis

**AI Evaluation**
- Get instant analysis of trade fairness
- See projected impact on your team
- View value comparisons between players

**Expert Rankings**
- Compare players using expert consensus rankings
- See rest-of-season projections
- Understand positional scarcity

## Waiver Wire

### Browsing Available Players

**Player Search**
1. **Tap "Waivers"** in the bottom navigation
2. **Filter Options**: Position, team, trending status
3. **Sort Methods**: Projections, ownership %, recent performance

**Player Information**
- Ownership percentages across all leagues
- Recent performance trends
- Upcoming schedule and matchups

### Making Claims

**FAAB Leagues (Auction Budget)**
1. **Select Player**: Tap on the player you want
2. **Enter Bid**: Choose your bid amount
3. **Drop Player**: Select a player to drop (if roster is full)
4. **Submit Claim**: Confirm your waiver claim

**Priority Leagues**
1. **Select Player**: Choose your target
2. **Set Priority**: Arrange multiple claims in order
3. **Drop Player**: Choose who to drop
4. **Submit**: Send your claim for processing

### Waiver Management

**Tracking Claims**
- View all pending claims
- See your chances of success
- Modify bids before processing deadline

**Budget Management** (FAAB Leagues)
- Track remaining auction budget
- See league average spending
- Plan for high-value pickups

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**App Won't Load**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Force close and reopen the app
3. Update to the latest version in the App Store/Google Play
4. Restart your device

**Sync Issues**
1. Pull down on any screen to refresh data
2. Check if you're logged into the correct account
3. Log out and log back in
4. Clear app cache (Android) or reinstall (iOS)

**Draft Room Problems**
1. Ensure strong WiFi or cellular connection
2. Close other apps to free up memory
3. Don't switch apps during the draft
4. Contact support if picks aren't registering

### Performance Optimization

**Improve App Speed**
- Close background apps
- Ensure device has available storage
- Update to latest iOS/Android version
- Clear app cache periodically

**Battery Life**
- Reduce notification frequency
- Lower screen brightness
- Use WiFi instead of cellular when possible
- Enable battery saver mode during long draft sessions

## Tips for Success

### Draft Strategy

**Preparation**
- Research player rankings and projections
- Understand your league's scoring system
- Plan for bye weeks and tough matchups
- Set up your draft queue in advance

**During the Draft**
- Stay flexible with your strategy
- Monitor position runs and scarcity
- Don't panic if your top targets are taken
- Consider trading draft picks

### Season Management

**Weekly Preparation**
- Check injury reports Tuesday and Wednesday
- Set lineups early, adjust as needed
- Monitor weather for outdoor games
- Stream defenses and kickers based on matchups

**Long-term Planning**
- Trade for playoff schedule advantages
- Handcuff your star running backs
- Keep an eye on rookie development
- Plan for keeper/dynasty decisions

### League Engagement

**Stay Active**
- Participate in league chat
- Propose fair trades regularly
- Be active on the waiver wire
- Celebrate good plays and commiserate bad beats

**Sportsmanship**
- Accept losses gracefully
- Don't spam trade offers
- Help newer players understand rules
- Keep trash talk fun and friendly
